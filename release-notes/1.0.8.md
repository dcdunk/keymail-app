## What's new in Keymail 1.0.8

**Your Sent and archived mail from every account**
Sent and Done now include mail from your iCloud, Yahoo and Outlook accounts, even emails you sent or archived in another app. Replies you sent elsewhere also show up in their conversations.

**Choose the name people see**
Each account now has a Name, the name people see when you email them. Add it in Settings → Accounts.

**Your alias, everywhere**
When an account sends from an alias, Keymail shows that address across the app, not the one you signed in with.

**A clearer Accounts screen**
Each account has its own card with labeled fields for Description, Name, Send from and Aliases. Typing in them is quicker too.

**Faster, friendlier search**
- Search keeps up as you type, even with lots of mail.
- Press / and start typing right away. The cursor is already in the search box.
- Click anywhere on the search bar to start typing.

**Fixes**
- iCloud inboxes with only older emails now show them instead of looking empty.
