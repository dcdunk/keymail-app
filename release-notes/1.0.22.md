## What's new in Keymail 1.0.22

**iCloud and Yahoo stay signed in**
When Apple's or Yahoo's mail server was busy, Keymail could mistake it for a wrong password and stop signing in, so your account looked signed out. Now only a truly refused password does that. A busy server is simply tried again a minute later.

Still signed out after resetting your Apple Account password? Apple cancels app passwords when your main password changes. Create a new one at account.apple.com and re-add iCloud in Settings → Accounts.

**Safer with AI assistants**
When an AI assistant asks to send an email for you, the approval card now lists every recipient and flags anyone who isn't already in the conversation. It also ignores the Send shortcut for its first second, so a quick keystroke can't approve it by accident.

**Safer invites and attachments**
Keymail only replies to a calendar invite automatically when it really comes from the organizer. Otherwise it asks you first. Attachments you save get the same Mac security check as any other download.
