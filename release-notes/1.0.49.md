## What's new in Keymail 1.0.49

**Undo and moving email**
- ⌘Z after deleting an email puts it back in your inbox again. Before, some undone emails ended up in Done instead.
- Undo puts an email back where it was. Deleting from Done and pressing ⌘Z returns it to Done, not the inbox.
- Pressing ⌘Z right after an action now always works, even if you're quick.
- Move to → Inbox works for emails in Trash and Done on every account.
- Deleting an archived iCloud email now moves it to Trash instead of letting it come back later.
- Deleting an email that's already in Trash tells you so instead of hiding it.
