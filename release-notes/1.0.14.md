## What's new in Keymail 1.0.14

**Clean up senders**
Press ⌘K and choose "Clean up senders" to see everyone who emails you, ranked by how much they send and how much of it you actually read. Unsubscribe and archive everything from a sender in one click, archive their mail but stay subscribed, or keep them off the list. Select several with X to clear them out at once, and ⌘Z puts archived mail back.

**Insights**
Press ⌘K and choose "Insights" for a look at your inbox over the last 7, 30 or 90 days: how much mail arrives, how much is from real people, what you never open, who emails you most and how quickly you reply. Senders you never read are one click from Clean up.

**Replies ready before you open the email**
Turn on "Write drafts ahead of time" in Settings → AI providers. When someone writes to you directly, your AI tool drafts a reply in your style, using your calendar when they ask to meet. Those emails get a Draft tag and gather in a new "Needs reply" split. Press R to edit the draft or ⌘↵ to send it. Nothing ever sends on its own.

**Search you can browse from the keyboard**
After you press Return in search, the arrow keys move through the results and ← → open the side panels. Press / to go back to the search box.

**Fixes**
Settings → AI providers now shows the AI tools you have installed instead of "Not installed".
