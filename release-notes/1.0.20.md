## What's new in Keymail 1.0.20

**New mail drops out of the notch**
On a MacBook with a notch, new mail now slides out of the notch with the sender, subject and first line. Click it to open the email, or rest your pointer on it to keep it open. Prefer the usual banners? Settings → General → Notification style.

**Turn off the sound**
Settings → General → Play a sound. Works for both notification styles.

**Email buttons, reorganized**
Reply, Done, Remind and the rest now sit above the email instead of below it, in clear groups: replying, dealing with the email, and a new AI group with Instant reply and Summarize. Send to Claude Code sits on its own at the right.

**Hide the shortcut keys**
Know the keys by heart? Settings → Appearance → Show shortcut keys tidies the email buttons. Hover any button to see its key.
