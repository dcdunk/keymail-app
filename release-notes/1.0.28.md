## What's new in Keymail 1.0.28

**Mark as junk**
- Send unwanted email to Spam with the new Junk button above any email, or press ! on your keyboard.
- It's also in the right-click menu, under Move to, in the ⌘K menu, and you can drag an email onto Spam in the sidebar.
- Changed your mind? ⌘Z puts it back in your inbox.
- Marking an email as junk helps your email provider catch similar mail in the future.

**Spam shows everything**
- The Spam folder now shows junk mail from iCloud, Yahoo and Outlook, not just Gmail.
