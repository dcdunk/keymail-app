## What's new in Keymail 1.0.25

A big round of fixes from a review of the whole app.

**Drafts you can count on**
- Closing a draft now asks whether to save it or throw it away.
- The Drafts folder shows drafts from iCloud and Outlook, not just Gmail.
- Drafts no longer fail with "signed out" when your account has a sender name, or when one address is set up on two accounts.
- A failed send keeps your draft. Quitting during Undo Send sends the email first.
- Reopened drafts keep their attachments and the address you sent from.
- Clicking an email link anywhere on your Mac opens a new draft in Keymail.

**Smarter replies**
- Replies go where the sender asked: help desks, forms and mailing lists that set a Reply-To now get your answer.
- Reply never answers your own unsent draft by mistake.
- Pasting several recipients makes one chip each.

**Stays signed in, keeps syncing**
- A brief hiccup at Google or Microsoft no longer tells you to sign in again.
- iCloud is far more patient before signing you out.
- New Gmail emails can't go missing when Google is busy.
- Outlook keeps older emails that are still in your inbox.

**Safer**
- Links that pretend to go somewhere else always warn you first.
- Keymail won't unsubscribe through a forged email.
- The AI settings now say exactly what's sent when drafts are written ahead of time.

**Calendar**
- Editing an Outlook event keeps its full description.
- All-day events can be edited.
- Answering an Outlook invite updates your Outlook calendar.
- Multi-day events show on every day in Month view.
- Times are right on daylight saving days.

**Everyday polish**
- "Unread only" keeps the email you're reading in place.
- Split and Filmstrip open your first email right away.
- Right-clicking another email doesn't change what you're reading.
- The top bar fits with both side panels open.
- Counts now agree with each other.
