## What's new in Keymail 1.0.44

**Security**
- Emails are more tightly contained: they can't send their own view to another page, and remote content stays blocked even while Keymail is still starting up.
- Stricter checks on links, meeting links, unsubscribe requests, sender logos and attachment file names.
- Remote images and unsubscribe requests are no longer saved to disk.
- Sign-in only completes on your email provider's genuine response.

**Speed and reliability**
- Long emails open and redraw faster, and saving your data no longer holds up the window.
- Background tools now time out instead of getting stuck.
- A full disk can no longer crash Keymail while it writes its log.
