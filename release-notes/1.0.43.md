## What's new in Keymail 1.0.43

**Reliability**
- If Keymail stops responding for more than a few seconds, it now keeps a short note about what was on screen, to help track down the cause. To see it, press ⌘K and choose Show freeze log.
- The note stays on your Mac and is never sent anywhere. It holds app details and an ID for the open email, but no addresses, subjects or message text, and it's deleted when you sign out of an account.
