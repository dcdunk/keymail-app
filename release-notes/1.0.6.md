## What's new in Keymail 1.0.6

**Keep your folders open while you work**
The folders sidebar now sits beside your inbox instead of covering it, just like the panel on the right.

**You decide when panels show**
In Settings → General, the Show folders sidebar and Show context panel switches now decide how each panel behaves:
- **On:** the panel is always there. The arrow keys move you in and out of it but never close it.
- **Off:** open and close it yourself. Press ← for folders or → for the context panel. The folders close again after you pick one.

**Smoother arrow keys in the folders**
- Press ← to move into your folders, starting on the one you're in.
- Use ↑ and ↓ to browse. A highlight ring shows where you are, and nothing opens until you press Return.
- Press → or Esc to go back to your mail without opening anything.
