## What's new in Keymail 1.0.21

**Right-click any email**
Right-click an email in the Split layout for Reply, Reply all, Forward, Mark as unread, Star, Remind me, Mute, Delete and more. Before, this only worked in the List layout.

**Better on narrow and vertical screens**
On a narrow window or a monitor turned on its side, the email buttons tuck away their names and shortcut keys so they fit, and Settings no longer runs off the right edge of the window.
