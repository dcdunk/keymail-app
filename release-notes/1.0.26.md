## What's new in Keymail 1.0.26

**See which account an email is in at a glance**
- Each account now has its own color. It shows next to every email in your inbox, in the email header, in the account menu and on the From line when you write.
- Pick any account's color in Settings → Accounts.

**See everyone on an email**
- Click the "to" line on an email to see every recipient with their address. Click again to shrink it back.

**Fixes**
- Emails from App Store Connect show Apple's logo instead of an unrelated one.
