## What's new in Keymail 1.0.9

**Your calendar, inside Keymail**
Press G then C, or click the calendar button in the top bar, to see your week. Switch between day, week and month, and press G then I (or the mail button) to get back to your inbox. Your Gmail, Outlook and Mac calendars all show together, and an event that appears in two places shows once.

**Fast from the keyboard**
- J and K move between events, H and L move between weeks, and T jumps to today.
- Press C and type an event in plain words, like "Lunch with Sam friday 1pm at Tartine". Keymail fills in the rest.
- Shift J joins the event's Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or Webex call.

**Everything about an event, in one card**
Click an event to see when and where it is, with a map. You can edit the title, time and notes, move it to another calendar, change the place with Apple Maps suggestions, get directions, or join the call.

**Answer invites before you open your calendar**
Invite emails now show the rest of that day, so you can spot a clash before you reply. Press Y, ? or N to answer yes, maybe or no.

**Email and events, linked**
An event shows the email it came from, and O opens it. Drop an email on the calendar button to set aside time for it.

**Make it yours**
Choose a color for each calendar, pick which day your week starts on, and choose where new events go in Settings → Calendar.

**Outlook calendars**
Outlook and Microsoft 365 calendars come straight from your Keymail account. Keymail asks for calendar access once, and your mail keeps working whatever you decide.
