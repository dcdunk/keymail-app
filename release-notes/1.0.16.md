## What's new in Keymail 1.0.16

**Smarter draft replies**
Drafts written ahead of time now go to emails from people, not companies. Delivery notices, account alerts and messages sent from company mailboxes no longer get a draft. Anyone you've emailed before always does.

**Drafts that fit the conversation**
A draft reply now sits where your reply would go: at the top when newest messages are first, at the bottom when oldest are first. It's the same size as the emails around it. Delete an email and its draft goes with it.

**Emails with a draft stay in your inbox**
An email with a draft shows in "Needs reply" and stays in your inbox too.

**Sent mail from anywhere**
Emails you send from the Gmail website or your phone now show in Keymail's Sent folder.
