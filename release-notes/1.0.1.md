## What's new in Keymail 1.0.1

**Settings look great in any size window**
Make the Keymail window as small as you like. Settings now uses the full window, so everything stays readable and easy to tap.
- Signatures show in full, with room to edit and preview them.
- Your accounts list keeps each address on one line, with tidy buttons to sign in again or remove an account.
- Gmail signature sync shows your Keymail and Gmail signatures one above the other, so long signatures are never cut off.

**One inbox, every time**
If you've turned off Important and Other, Keymail now opens straight to your Inbox, and "Go to Inbox" (G then I) takes you there too. No more empty screen after an update.
