## Keymail 1.0.1

### Settings fit any window size
- Settings now fills the whole window when it's narrow, instead of squeezing every section into a sliver.
- **Signatures:** in narrow windows the address list becomes a menu at the top, and the signature, editor and "In an email" preview keep a usable height. The section scrolls instead of squashing.
- **Accounts:** email addresses stay on one line. "Sign in again" and "Remove" are now compact icons (hover for their names), and the Add account buttons wrap neatly.
- **Inbox and Snippets:** headers and lists rearrange to fit narrow windows.

### Single inbox fixes
- With Important and Other turned off, Keymail now opens on your Inbox instead of an empty "Inbox zero" screen.
- "Go to Inbox" (G then I) also lands on the single Inbox.
