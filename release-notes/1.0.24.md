## What's new in Keymail 1.0.24

**Drag emails to folders**
Open the folders panel and drag any email onto Inbox, Starred, Done, Trash or one of your labels. The folder lights up as you hover, and Done or Trash can be undone with ⌘Z.

**Move to, from the right-click menu**
Right-click an email and choose Move to for the same places: back to the Inbox, Done, Trash, or a label.

Labels work on Gmail emails.
