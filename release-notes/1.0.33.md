## What's new in Keymail 1.0.33

**New inbox layout: Agentic (preview)**
- Your AI reads each email and suggests what to do with it, like writing a reply in your voice, starring it, archiving it or reminding you later.
- Nothing happens until you approve. Press ⌘↵ to approve, type a note to change the plan, or decline and handle the email yourself.
- You can edit the suggested reply before it goes out, and Undo Send still works.
- Turn it on in Settings → Appearance → Inbox layout.

**Easier to pick a layout**
- Settings now shows a small picture of each inbox layout, so you can see what you're choosing before you click.
