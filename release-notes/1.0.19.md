## What's new in Keymail 1.0.19

**Mail keeps arriving after your Mac sleeps**
Sometimes syncing kept spinning but no new mail showed up until you quit and reopened Keymail. This happened most after waking your Mac or switching Wi-Fi with an iCloud or Yahoo account. Keymail now notices a connection that's gone quiet, tries that account again a minute later, and keeps your other accounts up to date in the meantime.
