## What's new in Keymail 1.0.13

**Safer iCloud and Yahoo sign-in**
If iCloud or Yahoo turns down your app password, Keymail now stops trying and asks you for a new one, instead of retrying in the background. Repeated failed sign-ins can get an Apple Account locked, so this keeps yours safe. Keymail also waits a minute before reconnecting when the connection drops.

**See which address got the email**
Under the sender, "to me" now shows the address the email was sent to, like "to me (you@icloud.com)", so you can tell your accounts and aliases apart.

**Cleaner emails**
The small "OBJ" box that appeared in some emails is gone.
