## What's new in Keymail 1.0.17

**Reply to any email in a thread**
Every email in a conversation now has its own reply button beside the date: Reply, Reply all or Forward that email, not just the newest one.

**5-day week**
Settings → Calendar → Days in a week: show Monday to Friday, or all 7 days.

**Calendar and side panel**
Today's events sit right under the person's details instead of at the bottom of the panel. The unread count on the mail button no longer covers the icon.

**Easier to read in dark mode**
Headlines in marketing emails that sat over small decorations stayed black on the dark background. They're now light and readable.

**Names in every language**
Some recipients showed as code like "=?UTF-8?B?…" when you replied. They now show their real names.

**Clearer text on see-through windows**
With the window mostly transparent, grey text is stronger so it doesn't fade into your wallpaper. The opacity slider also moves smoothly again.
