## What's new in Keymail 1.0.30

**Links and formatting in your emails**
- Select text while writing and a small bar appears with Bold, Italic and Link. ⌘B, ⌘I and ⌘K work too.
- Add a link with the words you want people to see, like "the plan" instead of a long web address. Typing "example.com" or "name@example.com" is enough.
- Click a link in your draft to change it or remove it.

**Folders open faster**
- Sent, Trash, Spam, Drafts and Starred show your mail the moment you click them. Newer mail from the server joins as it arrives.
- All your accounts are checked at once, so one slow account no longer holds up the rest.
- Outlook folders load several times faster.

**Clearer names in your inbox**
- The people you're emailing come first and "You" comes last, like "Corrie, You".
- Names written "Last, First" show the first name, and titles like "Ph.D." are left off.
