## What's new in Keymail 1.0.2

**Conversations are easier to read**
Each email in a conversation now shows just what that person wrote, not the whole thread repeated underneath. Want the earlier messages? Click ••• under any email. Works with replies from Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Yahoo and help desks.

Prefer the full history every time? Turn it on in Settings → General → Earlier messages in each email.

**Collapse any email in a conversation**
Click the top of any email, including the newest one, to fold it away. Click again to open it.

**Find your message order faster**
Search Settings for "newest" or "oldest" to jump straight to the option for showing the newest or oldest message first.
