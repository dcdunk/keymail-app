## What's new in Keymail 1.0.39

**New: Packages**
- See everything you've ordered in one place. Keymail reads the shipping emails already in your inbox and lists your packages as Arriving today, Running late, On the way and Delivered.
- Each package shows the store's logo, the carrier's logo, its progress and when it's expected. One click opens the carrier's tracking page, copies the tracking number or opens the email it came from.
- Open it from the new parcel button at the top of the window, with G then P, or from ⌘K. A badge on the button shows how many packages arrive today.
- Everything is read on your Mac. Nothing is sent to a tracking service, and fake "your package is on hold" emails are left out.
