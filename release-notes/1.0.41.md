## What's new in Keymail 1.0.41

**Packages**
- Refresh anytime: press R or the refresh button to check your email for updates right away.
- Add a package yourself: press N or the + button and enter a name, or just paste a tracking number and Keymail fills in the carrier. If a shipping email for it arrives later, the two become one package.
- Edit any package: press I or the pencil to change its name, store, tracking number, carrier, expected date or status. Your changes stay when new emails arrive, and you can undo them.
- Optional AI reading: when you refresh, your AI provider can read new emails about packages on the way and fill in delivery dates, tracking numbers and problems like delays or missed deliveries. Keymail asks first, it only runs when you refresh, and other emails aren't sent. You can change this in Settings → Packages.
