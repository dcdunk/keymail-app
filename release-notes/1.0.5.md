## What's new in Keymail 1.0.5

**New inbox layouts**
Choose how your inbox looks in Settings → Appearance → Inbox layout:
- **Split:** your email list on the left and the email you're reading on the right, like Apple Mail. Use J and K to move through your mail and the reading pane follows along.
- **Stream (Preview):** every email as a card with its first few lines, one after another. Press Space to read the whole email, and reply, mark done or remind right from each card.
- **Filmstrip (Preview):** one email at a time. Flip with the arrow keys and jump anywhere with the bar at the bottom, where blue marks show what's unread.

Stream and Filmstrip are previews. They're new and may still have rough edges.

**Write in your own window**
New emails and replies now open in their own window. Drag it anywhere, even onto another display, and it reopens where you left it.

Prefer replying right in the conversation? Turn on Settings → General → Reply inside the conversation.

**Smaller improvements**
- Press Delete in an empty To, Cc or Bcc box to remove the last address.
- The buttons in the compose window line up neatly.
