## What's new in Keymail 1.0.31

**Messages that stay out of your way**
- Messages like "Moved to Trash. ⌘Z to undo." now appear as a small note at the bottom of the window.
- Your inbox no longer jumps down when one appears or slides back up when it goes away, so you won't click the wrong email.
- They still close on their own, or press Esc or the ✕ to close them sooner.
