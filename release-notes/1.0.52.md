## What's new in Keymail 1.0.52

**Search**
- iCloud, Yahoo and Outlook searches now look through all your mail on the server, not just recent emails. Older emails you've archived or sent show up too.
- "Show more results" at the bottom of a search loads the next 50 matches. It's also in ⌘K.
- A slow search can no longer replace the results of a newer one you just ran.
