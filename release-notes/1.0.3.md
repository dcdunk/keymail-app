## What's new in Keymail 1.0.3

**Do everything from the keyboard**
Keymail now works end to end without touching the mouse.
- In a conversation: Space opens the next email, Shift-O opens or closes them all, and the period key shows earlier messages.
- Press R on a conversation where you've started a reply to pick up right where you left off.
- Writing an email: Shift-Command-Return for Send later, Shift-Command-F to pick who it's from, Shift-Command-C or B for Cc and Bcc, Shift-Command-S for signatures and Shift-Command-I for snippets.
- Press Command-K to open attachments, RSVP to invites, join meetings, load images and change common settings.
- Press Return to confirm a delete, and use the arrow keys to reach email matches while searching.
- Find every shortcut in Settings → Hotkeys.

**Back to your inbox when you're done**
When you finish your last unread email, Keymail takes you back to the inbox instead of opening the next one. Prefer the old way? Turn it off in Settings → General.

**Fixes**
- Emails to some contacts wouldn't send, showing "Invalid To header". They now send normally.
- The Done folder now shows the emails you archive right away, instead of looking empty while it loads.
